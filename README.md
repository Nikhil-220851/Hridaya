# ♡ HRIDAYA – A Little From You

### Connecting Humanity Through Meaningful Acts of Giving

**HRIDAYA** is a unified donation and resource-sharing platform designed to bridge the gap between people willing to help and institutions that genuinely need support.

Orphanages, old-age homes, and other care institutions often require essential resources such as groceries, clothing, stationery, medicines, and daily necessities. At the same time, restaurants may have suitable surplus food, and individuals are willing to contribute but lack a reliable way to identify genuine needs.

HRIDAYA brings institutions, individual donors, restaurants, and administrators together on a single platform to make donations more accessible, organized, transparent, and trackable.

### 🌟 Key Features

- **Unified Donation Platform:** Coordinate food, clothing, groceries, stationery, and other essential resources through one platform.
- **Verified Institutions:** Enable institutions to register, submit documents, and undergo physical verification before publishing their requirements.
- **Surplus Food Redistribution:** Connect restaurants with verified institutions that require food, helping suitable surplus food reach those in need.
- **Smart Matching:** Rank eligible food donations based on distance, urgency, quantity suitability, and collection time using a rule-based matching mechanism.
- **Celebrate with Purpose:** Encourage birthday and special-occasion contributions through meaningful donations and approved celebrations.
- **Multi-Donor Fulfilment:** Allow multiple donors to contribute towards a single institutional requirement while tracking fulfilled and remaining quantities.
- **Transparent Tracking:** Track contributions through delivery and receipt confirmation.

### 🔄 How It Works

1. Institutions register and submit their verification documents.
2. Administrators review applications and assign field officers.
3. Institutions undergo physical verification.
4. Verified institutions publish their actual requirements.
5. Individuals contribute essential non-food resources, while restaurants list suitable surplus food.
6. The platform checks food safety and eligibility before matching.
7. Institutions accept suitable contributions.
8. Donations are delivered and receipts are confirmed.

### 🛠️ Technology

- **Database:** MongoDB
- **Authentication:** JWT and bcrypt
- **Security:** Role-based access control, HTTPS/TLS and audit logging
- **Document Management:** Private document storage with database references

### 🎯 Our Vision

To build a connected and trustworthy ecosystem where every genuine need can find a helping hand, every contribution has a purpose, and even a little from one person can make a meaningful difference.
